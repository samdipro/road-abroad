Rules

  • One main thing at a time. Spreading too thin is the biggest risk.
  • Shipped beats perfect. A live project with users beats a polished one nobody uses.
  • AI at work beats AI on the side. "Shipped AI in a regulated company" is my strongest story.
  • Referrals beat cold applications. Foreign hires often come in through referrals.
  • A cert alone won't get me hired. Proof of work and passing interviews will.
  • Rejections are normal. Many are about visas or quotas, not me.
  • The first title doesn't have to say "AI". Full-stack on an AI team is a win.
  • Check official visa rules before setting deadlines. Salary and age rules change every year.
